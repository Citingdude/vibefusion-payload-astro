# Vibefusion business context

This document is the source of truth for the business, brand, and content decisions that guide website work.

## Positioning

Vibefusion is a small digital studio that creates websites with character. The brand helps organisations turn an initial idea into a digital experience that people remember.

- **Core promise:** websites that feel like the client's brand.
- **What Vibefusion combines:** clear strategy, strong design, and smart technology.
- **Client outcome:** an online home base they are proud of — one that earns trust and can grow with their organisation.
- **Tone:** warm, capable, direct, and collaborative. Keep language human and concrete; avoid technical jargon unless it is useful to the client.
- **Team framing:** a small team for big ideas.

## Services

Vibefusion's offer should be explained as one connected process rather than separate, disconnected disciplines:

1. **Clear strategy** — clarify the story, objectives, and ideal customer so every decision has direction.
2. **Design with feeling** — make the identity and website recognisable, credible, and easy to use on every screen.
3. **Technology that moves with you** — build a fast, accessible, maintainable foundation that can grow without burdening the client with technical complexity.

## Homepage narrative

The homepage should follow this decision journey:

1. **Hero:** establish the promise and invite visitors to start a project or view the work.
2. **Services / approach:** immediately explain how Vibefusion delivers that promise through strategy, design, and technology.
3. **Work / cases:** demonstrate the promise with proof and outcomes.
4. **Process:** show what collaboration looks like and reduce uncertainty.
5. **About / trust:** introduce the people and working values behind the studio.
6. **Contact CTA:** provide a clear, low-friction next step.

## Visual direction

- The identity uses a distinctive, calm purple as the brand colour (`#6666cc`) with purple-tinted neutrals.
- The visual system should feel editorial and considered: confident typography, generous whitespace, soft rounded shapes, and restrained brand-colour accents.
- Headings use Alegreya; body copy uses Alegreya Sans.
- Favour semantic design tokens (`primary`, `text-muted`, `bg-elevated`, etc.) over hard-coded palette values in components.

## Information architecture

Primary navigation currently covers Home, Websites, Cases, Werkwijze, Over ons, and Contact. New content should reinforce this structure and use Dutch as the default site language.

## Content still needed

Do not invent these as facts. Collect them before making prominent claims on the website:

- ideal client types, sectors, and geography;
- case studies, including challenge, solution, and measurable outcomes;
- team member bios and photos;
- packages, starting prices, or engagement model;
- testimonials, client logos, awards, and other proof points;
- legal business details, social links, and contact preferences.
